# Local Cinema design rules

- **Mandatory Visual Identity Consistency:** Every new screen, settings menu, icon, file browser, dialog, or feature MUST strictly follow the approved Local Cinema visual identity — **Soft Black (`#171717`)** for primary surfaces, **Gold (`#E5A93B`)** for focus rings, selection, and primary actions, and **White (`#FFFFFF`)** for primary text and legible controls — with consistent proportions, spacing, and D-pad focus rings. Include a label or icon for status in addition to color.
- **Zero Focus-Ring & Label Clipping Rule (Strict):** On Android TV and Leanback, it is strictly forbidden for any edge of a gold D-pad focus ring (`#E5A93B`) or any label/caption below cards to disappear or be clipped during focus scaling or scrolling. Always set `clipChildren="false"` and `clipToPadding="false"` on scrollable rows, grids, and card wrappers, and provide sufficient container padding (`>= 10dp` top, bottom, start, end) and row height to accommodate focus scaling cleanly.
- **D-Pad Ergonomics:** Make every action reachable by D-pad with predictable directional order, visible focus after scrolling, and focus restoration when closing dialogs. Provide readable typography and spacing for a living-room viewing distance.
- **Visual Verification Gate:** No UI or feature task may be marked complete or handed off until the visual design is fully finished and verified on-screen on the actual target device.
- **Bilingual RTL/LTR:** Ship every label, message, dialog and accessibility description in English and Arabic. Use Android string resources with matching placeholders, start/end alignment, and bidirectional formatting for mixed Latin and Arabic text. Check mirrored navigation and content in Arabic RTL and English LTR.
- **Tokens & Touch Targets:** Reuse the owning app's design tokens and controls. Keep phone touch targets at least 48dp and leave safe space for the keyboard, system bars, and app navigation.
- Apply these project rules to skill search results and generated design-system suggestions.
